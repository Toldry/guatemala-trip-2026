The whole trip now has about 22–24 hours of driving in the last six days, so the final week leans heavily on the car.

### Dec 25 – Drive Xela → Lanquín, or Cobán if you start late
Christmas Day roads are quiet, which suits the longest drive of the trip. The route goes east past Guatemala City, then north into Alta Verapaz: green, rainy, mountainous Q'eqchi' Maya country of cloud forest and cardamom and coffee farms. Cobán is about 7 hours from Xela; Lanquín is about 1.5–2 hours further on a winding road. If you won't reach Lanquín by about 17:00, sleep in Cobán.

### Dec 26 – Semuc Champey
**What it is:** a 300 m natural limestone bridge over the Cahabón River. The river disappears underneath it, and on top lies a staircase of turquoise pools you can swim in. Climb about 30–40 minutes to the **El Mirador** viewpoint first, then swim. Nearby, the **K'an Ba caves** are a wade-and-swim tour by candlelight.

- **Getting there:** the last 10 km from Lanquín are very rough. Park in Lanquín and take a local 4×4 pickup.
- **Linguistics:** the name is Q'eqchi', usually glossed "where the river hides under the stones".

### Dec 27 – Drive Lanquín → Flores (about 6 h)
North through Raxruhá and Sayaxché, where a small car ferry crosses the Río La Pasión. I'm about 70% sure all of this road is paved now; ask in Lanquín.

**Flores:** a tiny island town on Lake Petén Itzá, joined to the mainland by a causeway. It was the last place the Itzá Maya held out against the Spanish, until 1697. Stay here, or in **El Remate**, about 30 minutes closer to Tikal.

### Dec 28 – Tikal
Arrive when the park opens. Climb **Temple IV**, then wander the **Gran Plaza**. A local guide is worth it for the first few hours. Bring 2–3 L of water each and insect repellent.

### Dec 29 – Yaxhá
Quieter than Tikal, between two lagoons. Climb **Temple 216** for sunset. After sunset, the last ~10 km back to the main road are a dirt track in the dark, so stay at a lodge near Yaxhá or join a sunset tour from Flores.

### Dec 30 – Drive Flores → Guatemala City (about 8–9 h)
Leave at dawn. The main paved highway runs south via **Río Dulce**, a river town with a 17th-century Spanish fort (Castillo de San Felipe). Sleep near the airport.

*A gentler finish:* split the drive with a night in Río Dulce, with a boat trip through the jungle gorge to **Livingston**, a Garífuna town on the Caribbean.

### Dec 31 – Fly home

## What changed from version 2
- **Gained:** Semuc Champey, and no rush to return the car or catch domestic flights.
- **Lost:** the Pacaya/rest day on Dec 30, and most of your spare time. If something goes wrong, Yaxhá is the easiest thing to cut.
- **Car:** a high-clearance SUV matters even more now (Lanquín roads, the dirt road to Yaxhá).
- **Book soon:** rooms in Lanquín and in Flores or El Remate over Dec 25–29.
