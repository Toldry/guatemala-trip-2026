## Car rental — key findings

### Guatemalan desk rules (Enterprise / National / Alamo franchise)

Alamo, National and Enterprise at GUA are all run by the same franchisee,
**Autos de Alquiler, S.A.** Source:
https://www.nationalcar.com/en/car-rental-locations/gt/la-aurora-intl-airport-k603.html

- CDW-TP is **mandatory** unless you show written proof of credit-card cover
  valid in Guatemala, or buy the National Protection Package (NPP).
  The CDW deductible is up to $2,500 depending on car class; the theft deductible is $3,500.
- TPL is **mandatory** unless you show proof of liability cover or buy NPP.
  TPL bought on third-party travel websites, and credit-card TPL, are not accepted.
- **NPP** bundles CDW-TP, tyres and windscreen, deductible protection and TPL.
  It has no deductible.
- Additional driver: **$10/day**. The driver must appear and sign at the counter
  and can only be added there.
- Ages 21–24 pay a young-driver daily fee (amount not published).
- Contract clause 8m: no driving on "obvious bad roads". Cover can be voided,
  so take the pickup shuttle Lanquín → Semuc Champey.

### Other quotes (rental only, desk cover extra)

- DiscoverCars, Alamo Yaris Cross: $649.66–685.85. This is the same franchisee,
  so mandatory CDW + TPL at the desk (~$43/day) bring it to ≈ $1,500 with a $1,500 excess left.
- Hertz, Toyota Raize or similar: $993.09 estimated. Cover is sold only at the counter.
- EconomyBookings: Alamo Yaris $366, Corolla Cross $741. The Costa Rent S-Presso
  is too weak for this route.
- Costa Car SUVs ($498): desk closed at 22:00, $2,500 deposit — rejected.

### Insurance conclusions

- HaFenix (Phoenix) rental excess add-on: $8/day, refunds the excess up to
  $2,000, once per policy, ages 24–75. It **does not** replace the mandatory desk
  CDW/TPL in Guatemala, so CDW + TPL + HaFenix (≈ $996) costs more than NPP (≈ $680)
  and covers less. Verdict: skip it.
- DiscoverCars "Full Coverage" ($10.12/day) reimburses after the fact and does
  not replace CDW (their T&C §9.4). Not needed with NPP.
- Notify HaFenix of the Central America trip: traveld@phoenix.co.il,
  WhatsApp 054-989-3455.
